## The list

1. **Local presence.** Offices, partners, or a visit schedule you keep.
2. **Relationships.** With retailers, distributors, buyers, institutions, built over years.
3. **Local team.** People who understand the language, the culture and the way business is done.
4. **Market intelligence.** Competitors, pricing, regulation, behaviour, channels, trends.
5. **Commercial network.** Knowing who decides and how each company operates.
6. **Reputation.** Being recognised as a long-term partner, not an occasional supplier.
7. **Business development.** Creating opportunities before they officially exist.
8. **Infrastructure.** Logistics, compliance, warehousing, legal and financial capability.
9. **Continuity.** Returning consistently, not only when there is an opportunity.

## Five phases

Understand, build, enter, develop, establish. The pillars are what you need; the phases are the order in which you build them.

## Why this framework

Because it makes the difference between export and internationalization checkable. If a plan has fewer than seven of the nine, it is an export plan. There is nothing wrong with exporting. It is just not the same project.
