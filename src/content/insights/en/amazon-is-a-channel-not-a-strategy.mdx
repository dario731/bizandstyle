## The marketplace trap

A marketplace listing is the fastest way to have a product available in the United States. It is also the fastest way to have a product invisible, discounted and dependent on a single algorithm.

Brands that treat the marketplace as the strategy end up competing on price with resellers of their own product. Brands that treat it as a channel use it for what it does well: availability, reviews, data.

## One inventory, several channels

The practical unlock is warehousing. When stock sits in Miami, the same inventory can serve marketplace orders, a direct-to-consumer store, retail replenishment and re-export to Latin America. Channel decisions become commercial decisions instead of logistical ones.

## What a distribution plan includes

- Domestic retail: department stores, chains, drugstores, specialty.
- Travel retail: duty-free, cruise lines, in-flight.
- Direct: a brand store with its own acquisition.
- Marketplaces: Amazon and category-specific platforms.
- Latin America: through the bonded hub, without paying U.S. duty on goods that leave again.

## Sequencing

The sequence is category-specific, and it is where experience matters. The mistake is not choosing a marketplace. The mistake is never choosing anything else.
