## Four things in one place

Cities compete on one or two advantages. Miami has four that matter for business development, and they reinforce each other.

## Decision makers

For decades the headquarters of Latin American operations for multinationals sat here, and the executives who run the region still come through Miami every month. Meetings that would take four flights happen in one week.

## Capital

Banks, funds and family offices from the whole hemisphere are represented. It has earned the nickname of Wall Street South, and the arrival of firms from New York and Silicon Valley has only deepened it.

## Logistics

PortMiami and Miami International Airport form the cargo gateway of the Americas. Add a bonded warehouse and the city becomes a redistribution point: hold goods in bond, release into the U.S. when it makes sense, re-export to Latin America without U.S. duty.

## Lifestyle

High-net-worth individuals move here, and where they live, they invest and do business. That is the bridge between commerce and the luxury economy that most cities never build.

## Why it matters for you

Being on the ground in Miami is, in itself, a decisive advantage. It is why BIZ & STYLE has been headquartered here since 2017, and why every capability we offer starts from this address.
