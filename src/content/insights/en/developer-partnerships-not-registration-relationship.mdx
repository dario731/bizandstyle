## The baseline

Every brokerage can register a buyer with a development. It is necessary and it is not a strategy.

## What a programme looks like

- A dedicated development page inside an acquisition engine we own and run.
- Google, Meta, SEO and GEO with international targeting, because the buyer of Miami rarely lives in Miami.
- A CRM that nurtures an international buyer over the months a decision actually takes.
- Events and visibility where the industry meets.
- Introductions inside an ecosystem, aviation, yachting, design, media, entertainment, that serves the same client the development does.

## Selective by design

Programmes like this only work with a few developments at a time. That is a feature. Selective, strategic, long term.

## The proof

Proprietary digital acquisition generated high-net-worth opportunities in some of the most relationship-driven segments of the Miami market before we offered the system to anyone else. We would rather show that in a meeting than claim it on a page.
