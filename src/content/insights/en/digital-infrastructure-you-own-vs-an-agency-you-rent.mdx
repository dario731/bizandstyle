## Two different purchases

Renting an agency buys activity: campaigns, posts, reports. Owning infrastructure buys capability: the ability to generate demand tomorrow without anyone's permission.

Both are legitimate. Only one of them compounds.

## What ownership means in practice

- The website is yours, fast, indexable, built to convert.
- The ad accounts are yours, with history and learning intact.
- The CRM is yours, with every lead's source, campaign, persona and intent recorded.
- The data is yours, so attribution is possible and budget decisions are evidence-based.

## Why it matters more abroad

A company entering the United States without its own digital infrastructure depends on whoever it hired to explain what is happening. With its own infrastructure it can see the market directly: which cities, which searches, which messages.
