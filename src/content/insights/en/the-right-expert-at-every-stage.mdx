## The principle

We execute what we have operating expertise in: strategy, internationalization, commerce, distribution, digital growth, business development, luxury real estate. For everything regulated, we work with carefully selected licensed professionals. Every engagement is in the right hands.

## The sequence for a U.S. entry

1. Entity: a corporate lawyer forms the company and obtains the EIN.
2. Banking: a banking partner opens the business account, also for non-residents.
3. Contracts: distribution, representation and employment agreements, drafted and reviewed by lawyers.
4. Tax: an accountant sets up bookkeeping and filings.
5. People: an immigration attorney designs the visa strategy for whoever relocates.
6. Trade: customs brokerage for the first shipments.

## What we do in between

We know the order, the timing and the dependencies. We brief each professional, keep the project moving, and make sure the commercial work does not wait for the paperwork.

## Why this is a feature

A platform that claims to do everything itself is either overreaching or hiding who really does the work. We would rather say it plainly: the right expert at every stage, and we know when to bring them in.
