## Three traits

A workflow is a candidate for AI when it is repetitive, mostly text, and produces a result someone can check. Most of sales administration and a surprising amount of operations qualify.

## In acquisition

Content systems that draft answer-shaped pages from verified facts. Campaign intelligence that reads search terms and suggests negatives. GEO checks that compare what the site says with what the web says.

## In sales

Lead enrichment on arrival. First-pass qualification against a rubric a human wrote. Follow-up drafts that a person edits and sends. Pipeline hygiene: which deals have not moved and why.

## In operations

Order and inventory exceptions surfaced before they become customer emails. Reporting assembled from the soft-ERP instead of from spreadsheets.

## Governance

What to automate, what to keep human, and how to measure it. We keep humans on anything that touches a client relationship or a regulated decision. The point is leverage, not replacement.

We use these tools inside our own divisions before recommending them. It keeps the advice honest.
