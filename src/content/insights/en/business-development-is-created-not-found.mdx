## Before it officially exists

The best deals we have been part of were not tenders. They were conversations that started a year earlier, with someone who was not yet looking, about a problem they had not yet named.

That is what business development means in practice: creating the opportunity, not responding to it.

## Relationships are capital

They accumulate slowly and compound. A network of more than two hundred commercial partners across the United States and Latin America was not assembled in a campaign. It was built one meeting at a time, and it keeps producing because it is maintained.

## What it requires

- Presence: physically, repeatedly, in the market.
- Memory: a system that remembers what was said and when to follow up.
- Generosity: introductions given before they are asked for.
- Patience: the timeline of trust is longer than a quarter.

## Discretion

Not every relationship belongs on a website. We showcase selectively and protect the confidentiality of clients and partners. It is one of the reasons the relationships last.
