## Thirteen steps, one loop

We describe the engine as a loop because that is how it behaves. Attention comes in, a conversation comes out, and what happened in between makes the next cycle better.

1. Intelligence: who buys, what they search, what they ask.
2. Positioning: what you say, in one sentence.
3. Website and landing pages: destinations built to convert.
4. Content, SEO and GEO: being the answer.
5. Google and Meta: paid demand, with international targeting.
6. Lead capture: forms that adapt to who is filling them.
7. CRM: the system of record.
8. Qualification: scoring by fit and intent.
9. Automation: the steps that should not depend on memory.
10. Nurture: email and retargeting until the timing is right.
11. Sales pipeline: stages that mirror how deals actually move.
12. Attribution: which click created which opportunity.
13. Optimisation: change what the data says to change.

## Where engines break

Usually between steps six and eight. Leads arrive, nobody scores them, the good ones wait behind the bad ones. Or attribution is missing, so budget goes to what feels productive rather than what is.
