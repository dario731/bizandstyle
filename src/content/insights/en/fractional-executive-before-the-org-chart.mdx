## The gap

Between "we have decided to enter the Americas" and "we have a team in the Americas" there is usually a year in which nobody senior owns the market. Junior staff do their best, the head office manages by email, and the opportunities that needed a decision on the spot go elsewhere.

## What a fractional mandate covers

- Commercial leadership: a head of business development for the region.
- Expansion leadership: one person owning the market-entry programme end to end.
- Project leadership: a launch, a repositioning, a channel build.
- Market representation: your company represented by a senior executive in Miami.

## Why it works better with a platform behind it

An individual fractional executive brings experience. One who arrives with a warehouse, a digital engine and a commercial network brings capability. The difference shows in the first quarter.

## The exit is the point

A good mandate makes itself unnecessary: the market grows, the internal organisation is built, the executive hands over a running structure. That is how we design them.
